# Services and Prices (Demo Data, Fictional)

Cavity treatment: from USD 150 per tooth. The cost depends on the depth of the decay and the filling material used.

Professional oral hygiene cleaning: USD 120. Includes plaque and tartar removal and polishing. Takes approximately one hour.

Teeth whitening: USD 350. Professional dental cleaning is required before whitening.

Dental implants: from USD 3,500 per implant. The exact cost is determined after consultation and diagnostic examination.

Dental prosthetics: from USD 900 per crown. The cost depends on the material.

Pediatric dentistry: a child's examination costs USD 60, and treatment of baby teeth starts at USD 100. We treat children aged 3 and older.

Initial doctor's consultation: USD 50. If the patient agrees to proceed with treatment, the consultation fee is deducted from the total bill.
