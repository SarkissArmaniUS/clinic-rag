Professional oral hygiene cleaning: USD 1,500. Includes plaque and tartar removal and polishing. Takes approximately one hour.

Teeth whitening: USD 4,500. Professional dental cleaning is required before whitening.

Dental implants: from USD 18,000 per implant. The exact cost is determined after consultation and diagnostic examination.

Dental prosthetics: from USD 6,000 per crown. The cost depends on the material.

Pediatric dentistry: a child's examination costs USD 400, and treatment of baby teeth starts at USD 900. We treat children aged 3 and older.

Initial doctor's consultation: USD 300. If the patient agrees to proceed with treatment, the consultation fee is deducted from the total bill.
